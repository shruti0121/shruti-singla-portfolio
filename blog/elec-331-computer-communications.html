ELEC 331(Computer Communications)

Shruti Singla – 31 December 2025

ELEC 331 introduced the fundamental concepts of computer communications and networking, covering the design, implementation, and analysis of modern computer networks. The course explored the Internet's layered architecture, communication protocols, and the principles behind reliable data transmission across interconnected systems.

Topics included application layer protocols such as HTTP, FTP, and SMTP, socket programming in Java, TCP and UDP, routing algorithms, flow and congestion control, Ethernet, local area networks, multiple-access protocols, and basic error detection and correction techniques.

ELEC 331 – Computer Communications focused on the architecture and operation of computer networks, emphasizing Internet protocols, reliable data transfer, routing, network programming, and link-layer technologies used in modern communication systems.
